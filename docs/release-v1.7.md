## What's new in 1.7: dynamic light

A new picture option, **Dynamic light** (Off, Soft, Strong), in the launcher's and the pause menu's **Effects** tab, with a live before/after preview:

- **The spirits, their magic and fire light up what is around them.** Dytto casts a cool blue-white light on the ground, a summoned spirit lights the grass it flies over, and torches, braziers and lava warm the floor and walls nearby.
- **The light falls on the scenery itself.** Lit ground keeps its own texture and colours, warmed or tinted by the light, instead of being covered by a glow. It falls off softly and never burns the picture out, however many lights are on screen.
- **Dark places dim a little so the light shows:** interiors, caves and dungeons. Daylight scenes are only ever lit, never darkened. The HUD and the lights themselves always stay at full brightness.
- Only real light sources glow. Ali, people, enemies, chests and the HUD give off no light, whatever their colours.

It works with every other effect (Lighting, HDR look, colour tone, internal resolution) on both the CPU and the graphics card, and costs about a millisecond a frame. The game itself plays exactly the same with it on.

**Settings moved:** to make room on the launcher's Effects tab, **Rumble** is now on the **Game** tab and **Show launcher at startup** on the **Speed** tab.

**Updating:** run the new Setup over your install. It keeps your settings, saves and save states. Dynamic light starts off.
