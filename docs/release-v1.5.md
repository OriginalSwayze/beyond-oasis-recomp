## What's new in 1.5: play in every language

Beyond Oasis came out in five languages, each on its own cartridge (sold as **The Story of Thor** outside North America). All of them now build into the same game, with every enhancement working in each:

| Version | Language |
|---|---|
| Beyond Oasis (USA) | English |
| The Story of Thor (Europe) | English |
| La Légende de Thor (France) | French |
| The Story of Thor (Germany) | German |
| The Story of Thor (Spain) | Spanish (its menus are in English, as on the cartridge) |
| The Story of Thor: Hikari o Tsugumono (Japan) | Japanese |

- **Language setting**, in the launcher's **Game** tab and the pause menu's **More** tab. In the pause menu it restarts in that version from its title screen (unsaved progress is lost).
- **Setup takes several ROMs:** pick or drop one from each country you own, and each is listed with its language. You no longer need the USA cartridge: any one version is enough.
- **Each version keeps its own saves,** save states and caches, so a save from one never loads into another. Your existing saves stay as they are.
- **European versions run as on a European console:** 50 frames a second, the taller 240-line picture and the PAL sound clocks. Widescreen, the HD effects, water and smooth motion all work there too.
- The launcher's case shows each version's own title, for example *La Légende de Thor*.

Also in this release, from 1.3.1 and 1.4: the water effect only on real water, the rushing wave filling the corridor in widescreen, and the redesigned launcher and pause menu.

**Updating:** run the new Setup over your install. It keeps your settings, saves and save states, and offers the ROMs it was built from; press **Add** to include more countries.
